Privacy policy

A quiet space for prayer and reading. Here is how Vigil handles your information, on your device and when you choose to go online.

At a glance

  • No Vigil account is required to pray or read.
  • Your settings and highlights are stored on your device.
  • Vigil does not include advertising or app usage analytics.
  • Downloads and external websites involve the services that provide them.

01 Information on your device

Vigil stores app preferences, reading settings, Bible highlights, reminder settings, and downloaded content locally so the app can remember your choices and work offline. This information is not sent to a Vigil account or cloud sync service.

Prayer reminders are scheduled on your device. If you enable notifications, your operating system manages the permission and displays those reminders.

Your device or operating system may include app data in backups, depending on your settings. Those backups are managed by your device or backup provider.

02 When the app goes online

Optional meaning search downloads

If you choose to download a meaning search pack, the app requests the necessary files from GitHub Releases and its download infrastructure. Once the pack is installed, meaning search runs on your device. Your search queries are not sent to GitHub for processing.

Information received by those services

Online requests expose your IP address and technical request information to the service handling the request. Those services handle that information under their own policies. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

When you open an external link, the destination website and your browser handle that visit under their own privacy settings and policies.

03 Community and support

Vigil uses GitHub Issues and Discussions for bug reports, questions, and feature ideas. Participation is optional and is handled through GitHub, which may require a GitHub account to post.

If you submit a report or discussion, the project maintainer can see your GitHub username and the information you choose to share, and may use it to respond and improve the app.

Posts are public. Please remove personal information from screenshots and logs. Do not post private prayer intentions, contact details, or other sensitive information.

04 Your choices and stored data

You can change your preferences and reminder settings in the app, turn off notification permission in your device settings, and remove optional search downloads through the app’s download controls.

Locally stored information remains on your device until you remove it or clear the app’s data. Uninstalling the app generally removes its local data; device backups may retain copies according to your backup settings.

GitHub posts are separate from app data. Deleting Vigil does not delete issues, discussions, comments, or attachments you have posted on GitHub. Manage those through your GitHub account and GitHub’s controls.

05 This privacy page

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, cookies set by this page. GitHub may process technical information needed to serve the page under its own privacy statement.

06 Changes and questions

This policy may change as Vigil develops. The date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.

For general questions about this policy, contact the maintainer through the Vigil GitHub community. Keep questions public-safe; do not include sensitive personal information.
